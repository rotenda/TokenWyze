Ondo Finance began in 2021 with a simple but ambitious question: why should institutional-grade financial products, US Treasury bills, money market funds, equities, ETFs, be available only to a narrow class of wealthy or connected investors? By June 2026, it has done more than anyone to answer that question in practice. Its TVL crossed $3 billion. It completed the first live cross-border tokenised Treasury settlement with JPMorgan, Mastercard, and Ripple. It offers over 100 tokenised US stocks and ETFs to non-US investors. And it is building Ondo Chain, its own purpose-built Layer-1 blockchain for institutional RWA settlement. The company was founded by former Goldman Sachs executive Nathan Allman, whose vision of democratising institutional finance through blockchain rails has become the defining product roadmap of the RWA tokenisation industry.

What Ondo Finance Actually Does

Ondo Finance operates at the intersection of traditional finance and decentralised finance. It takes real financial assets, primarily US government securities, money market instruments, stocks, and ETFs, and makes them available on blockchain networks as tokenised products. The tokens represent actual legal ownership in regulated financial instruments, not derivatives or synthetic exposures. When you hold OUSG, you hold a tokenised interest in a fund backed by BlackRock's BUIDL money market fund. When you hold USDY, you hold a yield-bearing token backed by short-term US Treasuries. When you hold a tokenised SPY through Ondo Global Markets, you hold an on-chain representation of an S&P 500 ETF.

The mechanism is important to understand. Ondo does not operate a custody service or a broker-dealer directly. It builds the tokenisation layer: the smart contracts, the legal documentation, the compliance controls, the distribution infrastructure, and the blockchain integrations that make institutional financial products accessible to a broader audience on-chain. The underlying assets are held by regulated custodians. The returns flow through to token holders automatically via smart contracts. The compliance controls are embedded in the token itself.

Ondo has evolved from a tokenised bond issuer into something closer to a full-service on-chain financial institution, one that offers custody, issuance, lending, derivatives, and settlement infrastructure within a single ecosystem.

The Infrastructure Strategy: Ondo Chain

In February 2025, Ondo announced Ondo Chain, a public proof-of-stake Layer-1 blockchain designed specifically for institutional-grade real-world asset settlement. This is a strategic inflection point. Instead of being a protocol that lives on top of Ethereum, Arbitrum, or XRPL, Ondo is building the infrastructure layer itself, a blockchain where the validator set comprises regulated financial institutions, combining the openness of a public blockchain with the compliance requirements of institutional finance.

Ondo Chain includes native proof-of-reserves oracles, ensuring that every tokenised asset on the network comes with continuous, on-chain verification of its backing. The DTCC's July 2026 tokenisation rollout places Ondo in a working group alongside BlackRock, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Nasdaq, and the NYSE, representing the most direct path to institutional-scale demand for Ondo's infrastructure. If the DTCC, which handles post-trade settlement for trillions of dollars annually, adopts Ondo Chain as a settlement layer, the implications for the entire RWA ecosystem would be transformative.

Understanding the ONDO Token

ONDO is the governance token of the Ondo Finance ecosystem. It is important to understand clearly what this means and what it does not mean. ONDO does not represent a direct economic interest in Ondo Finance's revenues or profits. It grants holders voting rights in the Ondo DAO, including control over treasury allocation, strategic initiatives, and ecosystem decisions. It also governs Flux Finance, Ondo's lending protocol, including control over lending parameters, collateral lists, and risk management.

A governance fee switch proposal, if approved, would route a portion of protocol fees to ONDO holders, creating a direct economic link between the platform's growth and the token's value. That proposal is under active discussion within the DAO as of mid-2026 and represents the most significant potential catalyst for ONDO token value beyond governance utility.

The supply headwind investors must understand

Ondo has a maximum supply of 10 billion tokens, with approximately 4.87 billion currently circulating. A significant unlock event in January 2026 released approximately 1.94 billion tokens worth roughly $655 million at the time, sending ONDO down approximately 10%. A further 6-plus billion tokens are scheduled for unlock through 2029, representing a structural supply increase of approximately 133% of current circulating supply. This is the most important risk factor for any investor considering ONDO: the token's fundamental strength in institutional adoption must outpace consistent selling pressure from scheduled unlocks over the next three years.

The Competitive Position

Ondo's primary competitors in the tokenised RWA product layer are Franklin Templeton's Benji fund, BlackRock's BUIDL, and emerging platforms like Superstate and OpenEden. What distinguishes Ondo is the breadth of its product suite: it is the only platform offering tokenised Treasuries, tokenised stocks and ETFs, yield-bearing dollar tokens, on-chain derivatives, and its own Layer-1 blockchain under a single ecosystem umbrella.

The hiring of John Hoffman, former ETF chief at Invesco and managing director at Grayscale, to lead Ondo's tokenised portfolio products signals the next product direction: diversified, programmable tokenised investment baskets that mimic ETF structures on-chain. This moves Ondo from single-asset tokenisation toward portfolio construction on-chain, a significantly larger addressable market.
